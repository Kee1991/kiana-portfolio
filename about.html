01 / ABOUT

A career rooted in service.

Kiana C. Farlow is the Founder and Executive Director of Law Enforcement Mentor Club Corporation, a Philadelphia-based nonprofit supporting youth and families through mentorship, education, music, sports, and the arts.

As a former Philadelphia police officer with 13+ years of experience, Kiana brings firsthand knowledge of public safety, community engagement, youth development, and wellness. She builds bridges among residents, police, schools, businesses, faith-based organizations, and civic leaders.

Her leadership philosophy is simple: we are stronger together. Through organized action and trusted relationships, she turns community needs into pathways for opportunity and public trust.
