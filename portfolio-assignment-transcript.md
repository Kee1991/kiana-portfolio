# Portfolio Assignment — Request and Revision Transcript

## Project purpose

Create a professional student portfolio website for Kiana Farlow that communicates public-service leadership, community impact, responsible AI and public-sector learning, professional experience, and future work to employers, business partners, and mission-aligned organizations.

## Requested website elements

- Professional identity: Kiana Farlow, Executive Director of Law Enforcement Mentor Club Corporation, Community Engagement and Law Enforcement Wellness Champion, and former Philadelphia police officer with 13+ years of experience.
- Concise professional introduction, longer personal bio, leadership philosophy (“We are stronger together”), public-service interests, and simple navigation.
- Professional headshot and a community-mentorship image integrated into the design.
- Resume/CV link, LinkedIn link, business email, and personal email.
- Working links to the Law Enforcement Mentor Club Corporation website and PayPal donation page.
- Pink, navy, pale blue, gray, and gold visual palette aligned with the resume and a polished, executive-friendly layout.
- Instrumental background music and an interactive hands-together community-building graphic, with browser-friendly controls and a respectful fallback for autoplay restrictions.
- Featured media coverage for peace marches and anti-violence events planned, led, or facilitated by Kiana.
- Impact section with measurable outcomes and an executive-friendly visual chart.
- Case studies covering peace marches, nonprofit leadership, community policing and wellness, and the Make It Out Boot Camp.
- A course-learning section for SAS GAFL 5420: AI for Public Sector Leadership.
- A project-workspace link and a clearly labeled future project: Officer Wellness Resource App.
- Footer identifying the site as a student portfolio.

## Evidence and content added

- Make It Out Boot Camp: first cohort graduated in August 2026 after a six-week program; 11 youth graduated; early survey results include a 4.73/5 average rating, 100% willingness to recommend, 81.8% feeling safe and comfortable, and 72.7% reporting more tools for life challenges. Results are labeled as pilot-sized and directional.
- Community policing and wellness: initiatives included Ball with Badges, Safe Block Party, Safe Halloween, and Water Ice and Chill with Cops; partnerships were strengthened with faith-based organizations, businesses, city leaders, residents, and police; the portfolio reports a 10% reduction in crime in a community of approximately 30,000 residents.
- Selected outcome indicators: 30K+ people and organizations reached, 20% increase in perceived safety, 10% crime reduction, $30K program budget managed, and 11 youth graduates.
